This Privacy Policy explains what personal information [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (“we”, “us”) collects through the Restyle app and related services (“Restyle”), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect

Account information. What we receive depends on how you sign up:

  • Email and password: your name, email address and password. We store a hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Google: your name, email address and profile picture URL from your Google account, plus the sign-in tokens Google issues to us.
  • Apple (iOS): your email address (or the private relay address Apple creates if you hide your email), your name if you choose to share it the first time you sign in, and the sign-in token Apple issues to us.

Photos and designs. The room photos you take or choose, and the AI-generated designs created from them. For each design we also store the room type, the style you picked and when it was created. Before uploading, the app shrinks each photo to a JPEG of at most 1536 pixels on its longest side.

Session and device information. When you sign in, we create a session that records an expiry time and may include the IP address and device/browser user-agent of the request. Like most online services, our hosting providers also process IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the Service.

Messages you send us. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. The app pre-fills the subject with the app version.

What we don’t collect. Restyle does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not request your location. The app does not read the metadata of your photos, such as location.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you choose “Take photo”, so you can photograph a room.
  • Photos: used only to let you pick a photo. Only the photo you select is uploaded.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. Restyle also offers a sample room photo, so you can try it without granting either permission.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To generate redesigns of your photos, upscale them to HD when you ask, and create share links when you tap Share.
  • To show your design history in “My Designs”.
  • To enforce and display the daily design limit.
  • To send password-reset emails you request.
  • To respond to support requests, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your photos or designs to train AI models.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run Restyle, and only as needed for the purposes above:

  • OpenAI receives your room photo and a text instruction based on your chosen room type and style, in order to generate your design. We do not send your name or email address to OpenAI. OpenAI processes API data under its own terms. [CONFIRM: under OpenAI’s API data policy, API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.]
  • ImageKit stores your original photos and designs as private files in a folder tied to your account, and creates resized, HD (AI-upscaled) and share-card versions.
  • Neon hosts our database (account, session and design records) and the server function that generates designs. It also powers the live “designs left today” counter.
  • Expo (EAS Hosting) hosts our app’s server endpoints.
  • Resend receives your name and email address to send password-reset emails.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you use those options, under their own privacy policies.

When you share. When you tap Share, you create a link to an image that shows your design, a smaller copy of your original photo, and the style and room type. Anyone with the link can view it until it expires, about one hour later, and can save a copy.

Legal and business transfers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Restyle. If Restyle is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

5. How we protect your information

  • Photos and designs are stored as private files. The app shows them through signed links that expire after one hour.
  • Every request for your designs is checked against your signed-in session. You can access only your own designs.
  • On iOS and Android, your session token is stored in the device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). On the web it is kept in an HTTP-only cookie.
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes, and password-reset links expire after one hour.
  • Connections between the app and our services use HTTPS.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information, photos and designs: until you delete your account. Restyle does not currently let you delete individual designs.
  • Sessions: they expire after a period without use, and all of them are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Password-reset requests: the links expire after one hour.
  • Support emails and server logs: [RETENTION PERIOD].
  • Our service providers: they may keep data for limited periods under their own policies, for example for abuse monitoring or backups.

  • Permissions: you can revoke camera and photo access in your device settings.
